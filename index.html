About Chavan Diagnostic Centre

Dedicated Healthcare Support for Our Afzal Gunj Community

Located on Jummerat Bazar Road near Purana Pool in Afzal Gunj, Chavan Diagnostic Centre serves as a convenient, one-stop neighbourhood destination for vital clinical tests, therapeutic physical rehabilitation, and professional vision care.

We understand that managing family health requires accessible, dependable support close to home. Instead of visiting separate clinics across the city, our patients can get fasting blood tests done early in the morning, receive targeted physiotherapy for acute pain or sports recovery, and get prescription spectacles or eye checkups in the same visit.

Our Healthcare Services

Convenient, high-quality diagnostic investigations, rehabilitation therapies, and prescription eyewear tailored to your needs.

1. Diagnostics Laboratory

Clinical pathology, biochemistry & routine tests

  • Blood Tests: Complete Blood Picture (CBP/CBC), ESR, Lipid Profile, Liver Function (LFT), Kidney Function (KFT/RFT), Serum Calcium.
  • Urine & Stool Tests: Routine Urine Analysis (Complete), Microscopic examination, Urine Pregnancy Test (UPT), and Stool routine.
  • Thyroid & Diabetes Screening: Fasting Blood Glucose (FBS), Post Prandial (PLBS), HbA1c (Glycated Hemoglobin), T3, T4, TSH panels.
  • Health Checkup Packages: Basic health check, diabetic health profile, master executive packages, and senior citizen wellness screening.
  • Home Sample Collection: (Confirm availability with centre for nearby areas in Afzal Gunj and Purana Pool).

* Complete list of specialized pathology tests available on request. Fasting guidance provided.

Book Lab Test

2. Physiotherapy Clinic

Pain management, injury recovery & mobility therapy

  • Back & Neck Pain: Targeted relief for cervical spondylosis, lumbar spondylosis, sciatica, disc slip symptoms, and chronic postural neck stiffness.
  • Joint & Knee Pain: Therapy for osteoarthritis, frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis), tennis elbow, heel pain (plantar fasciitis), and ankle sprains.
  • Sports Injury Rehab: Ligament strain rehab, muscle sprains, runner's knee, and conditioning to restore active range of motion safely.
  • Post-Surgery Rehabilitation: Graduated post-operative mobilization following fracture fixation, joint replacement, or orthopaedic procedures.
  • Neuro Physiotherapy & Exercise Therapy: Paralysis/stroke rehab support, Bell's palsy therapy, ergonomic guidance, and customized therapeutic exercise regimens.

* Tailored session plans designed in coordination with your consulting doctor's advice.

Book Physiotherapy

3. Optical Clinic

Vision testing, prescription eyewear & lenses

  • Comprehensive Eye Checkup: Objective and subjective refraction testing, visual acuity assessment, and eye power evaluation for all age groups.
  • Spectacles & Frames: Wide collection of rimless, semi-rimless, acetate, metal, and lightweight durable frames for men, women, and children.
  • Advanced Ophthalmic Lenses: Single vision lenses, bifocal lenses, modern progressive lenses, anti-glare (ARC), and blue-light screen protective filters.
  • Contact Lenses: Spherical and toric soft contact lenses, daily disposable and monthly wear options, along with lens cleansing solutions.
  • Sunglasses & Eye Accessories: UV-protective powered and non-powered sunglasses, lens cleaning kits, protective frame cases, and cords.

* Outside ophthalmologist and clinic prescriptions gladly fulfilled with precision lens fitting.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Common patient inquiries. Please confirm test-specific requirements with the centre.

Tests like Fasting Blood Sugar (FBS) and Lipid Profile generally require 8 to 12 hours of overnight fasting (water is allowed). Routine tests like Complete Blood Count (CBC) or Thyroid tests (TSH) often do not require strict fasting. Please confirm specific fasting guidelines for your prescribed test by calling us at 072070 72225.

Most routine blood and urine test reports (CBC, blood sugar, lipid panel, urine routine) are prepared and delivered on the same day. Specialized hormones or culture tests may take 24–48 hours. Digital reports can also be shared via WhatsApp for your immediate convenience.

Home sample collection is provided for elderly patients and individuals unable to travel in surrounding areas including Afzal Gunj, Rahimpura, and Purana Pool. Availability and scheduling vary based on morning technician routes; please call 072070 72225 to confirm and pre-book your slot.

We address chronic neck and lower back pain, sciatica, frozen shoulder, osteoarthritis knee stiffness, post-fracture joint stiffness, sports sprains, and neurological rehabilitation (such as post-stroke mobility recovery). Treatment includes manual mobilization, modalities, and home exercise guidance.

Yes, absolutely. We welcome all registered prescriptions from hospital doctors, ophthalmologists, and local general practitioners for lab testing and custom lens fitting. If you do not have an eye prescription, our optometrist can also perform a vision checkup on site.

The centre opens early at 7:45 AM from Monday onwards to cater to early morning fasting tests. Timings may vary during regional holidays and festivals such as Bathukamma and Sankranti. Please call 072070 72225 before visiting to confirm the day's exact closing hours.
